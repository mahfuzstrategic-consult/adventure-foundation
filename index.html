Bangladesh Adventure Foundation is a non-profit youth development organisation. We use travel, adventure and volunteer service to help young people build confidence, look after their mental health and give back to their communities.

Who we are

Youth first, adventure as the method

We believe young people learn the most outside their comfort zone. A trek through the hills, a week in a village or a day spent helping after a flood teaches planning, patience, teamwork and empathy in a way a classroom rarely can.

To promote adventure sports and activities among the youth of the country in order to build their personality and character by infusing in them a sense of discipline and national pride.Our founding objective · Memorandum of Association

Every programme we run is shaped by volunteers and open to young people from any background, anywhere in Bangladesh.

Our work

What we do

Six areas of work, all aimed at the same outcome: capable, healthy young people who care about the country and its people.

Training · Leadership

Youth development

Short and long courses in leadership, outdoor skills, first aid and social responsibility that prepare young people for work and community life.

Mental health · Motivation

Healing through travel

Group trips, nature walks and camps that give young people a break from pressure, time to talk, and a renewed sense of purpose.

Relief · Medical · Development

Volunteer social work

Volunteers support relief, medical and development projects alongside partner organisations, especially in remote and hill communities.

Surveys · Insight

Research & market studies

Youth-led field research on young people's needs, tourism and local markets, giving volunteers real research experience and partners useful evidence.

Hills · Rivers · Coast

Adventure & eco-tourism

Camping, hiking, river cruising, rowing and study tours, plus cultural tours and ethnic food festivals that celebrate the country's diversity.

Workshops · Publications

Seminars & knowledge

Workshops, seminars and roundtables at national and international level, plus books and media on Bangladesh's cultural and natural heritage.

The heart of the Foundation

Volunteers do the work

We are a volunteer-driven organisation. Students and young professionals plan trips, run training days, collect research data and turn up when communities need help. In return they gain skills, friendships and a record of real service.

Field volunteerTrips · Camps

Help lead groups on treks, camps and study tours.

Community responderRelief · Health

Join relief, medical and development drives with partner agencies.

Youth researcherSurveys · Data

Collect and analyse data for our research and market studies.

Peer supporterWellbeing

Be a friendly face for young people joining our wellbeing trips.

From our record

An adventure festival across the hills

Working with the Chittagong Hill Tracts Development Board, the Foundation helped run a five-day adventure festival across all three hill districts, bringing young people from around the country together with hill communities.

How we are governed

The Foundation is run by an elected Executive Committee and a General Body made up of its founding members and invited members. The Executive Committee sets our programmes and builds partnerships with organisations at home and abroad that share our objectives.
